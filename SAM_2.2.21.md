# SAM 2.2.21 — Digital Body Foundation

This release turns the SAM architecture into an explicit digital-body model while keeping one user-facing SAM personality.

- Brain: reasoning, planning, research and decision routing.
- Heart: artist and psychology specialists.
- Eyes/Ears: perception and voice capability slots.
- Memory: durable personal/knowledge memory.
- Hands: coding, tools, APIs and automation capability slots.
- Legs: navigation, app movement and device action capability slots.
- Specialist registry is provider-neutral; concrete AI providers can be attached without changing SAM's identity.
- The launcher UI now shows the body map.
- Sensitive actions remain confirmation-gated.
- Knowledge refresh remains opt-in/configurable and local memory is not model training.
